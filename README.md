# HerSpace 4.0 — React Application

A safe, confidential emotional wellbeing platform exclusively for women and transgender women aged 18+.

## Key Features & Business Rules
- **Dual-Tier Emotional Care**:
  - **Trained Human Listeners** (₹399 / 60 min session): Peer active listening, emotional first aid, stress and venting support.
  - **Professional Counsellors** (₹999 / 60 min session): RCI-certified psychologists for evidence-based psychotherapy (CBT, ACT, trauma-informed care).
- **HerSpace Live Melodies (Live Singers)**:
  - Private, soothing telephone vocal comfort sessions (Carnatic acoustic, Tamil lullabies/thaalattu, relaxing ragas) to ease insomnia, grief, or loneliness.
- **Client-Approved Onboarding & Membership Journey**:
  - Step 1: Free Registration with Indian mobile (10-digit), OTP verification, alias, and 18+ eligibility confirmation.
  - Step 2: 3-Slide App Tour detailing the tiers, masked calling, and wellbeing ecosystem.
  - Step 3: Complimentary 3-month Free Trial (₹0) plus 1 free 30-minute listening call voucher (valid for 7 days).
  - Step 4: Service Hub booking, transitioning directly into **My Personal Space** for returning members.
- **Exotel 2-Way Number Masking Telephony Bridge**:
  - Virtual bridge simulation with masked numbers, hold/resume, secure call parking & slot retrieval, counsellor transfer, and drop reconnection.
- **Women's Life & Wellbeing Knowledge Hub**:
  - 11 culturally grounded categories (A to K) with evidence-based guidance in English and Tamil (தமிழ்), bookmarking, and private Q&A submissions.
- **My Cycle — Optional Period Tracker**:
  - 100% opt-in menstrual cycle logging stored strictly on the user's device with zero third-party tracking, symptom tracking, JSON data export, and permanent wipe capabilities.
- **HerSpace Calm Audio Sanctuary**:
  - Web Audio synthesis engine delivering harmonic acoustic soundscapes, Tamil guided breath reset (Amaithi Moochu), veena melodies, rain ambience, and sleep timer.
- **100% Bilingual Localization**: Full instant toggle between English and Tamil across all screens.

## Technical Architecture
- **Framework**: React 19 SPA with Vite and TypeScript.
- **Styling**: Modular CSS design system preserving authentic typography (Bodoni Moda & Manrope & Noto Sans Tamil), layout, and responsive mobile drawer & bottom navigation.
- **Local Dev Server**: Vite on `0.0.0.0:3000`.
